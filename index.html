01 · Neuroscience

Can you balance through your tongue?

After losing her vestibular sense, Cheryl Schiltz used a helmet accelerometer and an electrode array on her tongue to stay upright. Some improvement even persisted after she removed the device.

02 · Genetics

Why are bee sisters 75% related?

Male honeybees are haploid; females are diploid. Full sisters with the same father share all his genes and, on average, half their mother's contribution. Real hives are more complicated because queens mate with multiple males.

Kin selection explains why helping relatives reproduce can be favored by evolution. But the 75% relatedness calculation alone cannot explain sterile workers, colony organization, or every form of altruism.

04 · Bonus

How does a bee give directions?

The waggle dance encodes distance through the duration of the waggle run and direction through its angle relative to vertical, representing the direction of flowers relative to the sun.

The big question

How does nature produce astonishing capabilities without anyone designing them? A brain repurposes sensory information; genetic incentives shape cooperation; and a colony encodes a location through movement.
